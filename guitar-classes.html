From First Chords to Stage Solos

Whether your dream is strumming soulful acoustic ballads by the campfire, executing intricate fingerstyle classical pieces, or shredding expressive electric guitar solos through tube amplifiers, our guitar curriculum turns ambition into effortless proficiency.

At Classic Music Institute in Sector 34-A Chandigarh, we eliminate the pain and frustration typically associated with early guitar practice. Our instructors focus on correct fretboard positioning, thumb mechanics, pick holding angles, and muscle memory training so you transition smoothly between open chords and barre chords.

Three Dedicated Guitar Specialization Tracks

Track 1: Acoustic Rhythm & Fingerstyle

Essential open chords (C, G, D, Em, Am), strumming patterns in 3/4 & 4/4, Travis picking, syncopated rhythms, pop accompaniment, and capo transposition.

Track 2: Electric Lead Guitar & Rock

Pentatonic and modal scales, alternate picking, string bending, hammer-ons & pull-offs, vibrato, slide guitar, amplifier tone settings, and Rockschool Grade exams.

Track 3: Classical Nylon-String Guitar

Nylon string ergonomic right-hand technique (P-I-M-A), arpeggios, Spanish flamenco rhythms, staff notation sight-reading, and Trinity Classical Guitar syllabus.

Track 4: Jazz & Advanced Harmony

7th, 9th, and 13th chord voicings, chord melody arrangements, 2-5-1 progressions, walking basslines with chord comping, and modal improvisation.

Why Learn Guitar at Classic Music Institute?

  • Guitars Available at Campus: No need to carry your instrument every day; play on our calibrated Fender, Yamaha, and Ibanez studio guitars.
  • Dual Tablature & Staff Notation: Read guitar tabs quickly while also understanding full musical sheet notation.
  • Jam Sessions & Band Ensembles: Periodic opportunities to play with student drummers, keyboardists, and vocalists.

Ready to Play Your First Song?

Schedule a free 30-minute private trial class with a guitar maestro at our Sector 34-A Chandigarh campus or live online.
