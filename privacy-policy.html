At Classic Music Institute (accessible at classicinstitute.com), safeguarding the privacy and security of our prospective students, enrolled pupils, and guardians is fundamental to our institutional integrity. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal information collected, how it is processed and protected, and your rights concerning your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information when you interact with our website, request a free trial lesson, submit an inquiry form, or enroll at our Chandigarh studio or online academy:

  • Personal Identifiers: Student name, date of birth, age, parent or guardian name (for minors under 18 years).
  • Contact Information: Phone number, WhatsApp contact, email address, and residential postal address.
  • Academic & Musical Information: Musical instrument or vocal genre of interest, prior experience level, goals, and exam registration data (for Trinity College London, ABRSM, Rockschool, or Gandharva Mahavidyalaya).
  • Technical Browsing Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and anonymous session telemetry to improve website performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by Classic Music Institute is utilized strictly for educational and administrative purposes:

  • To schedule, confirm, and conduct free trial auditions and assessments.
  • To allocate faculty maestos, studio rooms, and lesson slots according to student availability.
  • To facilitate registrations with accredited examination boards (Trinity, ABRSM, Rockschool UK, etc.).
  • To communicate lesson schedules, fee invoices, studio holiday closures, recital announcements, and student performance progress reports.
  • To process tuition fee payments and issue official receipts.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact information to third-party marketing companies.

3. Data Protection & Security

Classic Music Institute implements rigorous technical, physical, and administrative measures to protect student records. Our digital records are encrypted with SSL/TLS encryption. Physical student records at our campus located at SCO 64-65, 2nd Floor, Sector 34-A, Chandigarh are stored in secure administrative archives accessible only by authorized admissions personnel.

4. Minor & Children's Privacy

Given that we teach students from age 4 onwards, protecting childrenâ€™s privacy is paramount. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 18 without explicit parental or legal guardian consent. All enrollment contracts, fee transactions, and trial bookings for minors must be authorized by a parent or legal guardian.

5. Media, Video & Audio Recordings Policy

From time to time, Classic Music Institute records recitals, masterclasses, and studio sessions for educational review, student portfolios, or institute showcases. Written consent is obtained from adult students or parents of minor students prior to publishing any performance media on our official channels.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We may employ reputable third-party companies (such as payment gateways, SMS notification providers, and exam boards). These third parties only have access to specific data required to perform designated tasks on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

7. Contact & Data Officer
