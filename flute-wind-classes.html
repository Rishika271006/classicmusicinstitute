Flute & Woodwind Classes

Home / Services / Flute & Woodwinds
Bamboo Bansuri & Western Orchestral Flute

The Breath of Melody and Transcendence

The flute transforms the simplest breath into transcendent melody. From the meditative, pastoral tones of the Indian Bamboo Bansuri echoing classical ragas to the crisp, agile brilliance of the Western silver concert flute, this instrument connects mind, breath, and spirit like no other.

At Classic Music Institute in Sector 34-A Chandigarh, our wind program trains students in diaphragmatic breath support, lip embouchure focusing, finger pad sealing, and half-hole microtonal shading (Komal and Teevra notes).

Dual Specialization Options

Option A: Classical Indian Bansuri

Focuses on side-blown bamboo flute (E-bass / C-medium), Saral swaras, finger positioning without hand cramping, Alaap elaboration, Gamak, and Raagas like Bhupali, Yaman, and Bageshri.

Option B: Western Concert Flute

Boehm fingering system, lip-plate airstream focus, tone purity across 3 octaves, double tonguing, orchestral excerpts, and Trinity / ABRSM graded woodwind exams.

Option C: Breathing & Breathwork

Pranayama-inspired breath retention exercises, continuous tone sustaining, circular breathing foundations, and eliminating lightheadedness in beginner practice.

Option D: Melodic Improvisation

Fluid Meend glides, murki ornaments, film theme phrasing, fusion melodies with guitar/keyboard accompaniment, and recording studio mic techniques.

Discover Your Breath of Music

Book a free 30-minute 1-on-1 trial class with our woodwind maestro at SCO 64-65, Sector 34-A Chandigarh or online.
