Welcome to Classic Music Institute. By registering for lessons, booking a trial audition, or accessing our online and studio services via classicinstitute.com, you enter into a binding agreement with Classic Music Institute. Please review the following terms carefully before enrolling.

1. Admissions & Enrollment

Enrollment in any instrumental or vocal discipline is confirmed upon the completion of the student registration form, allocation of an available faculty slot, and payment of the designated tuition fee. Minors under 18 years of age must have their enrollment application signed or electronically acknowledged by a parent or legal guardian.

2. Complimentary Trial Audition Policy

Classic Music Institute provides one (1) complimentary 30-minute evaluation and audition per prospective student across any single discipline. Trial sessions are subject to instructor and studio availability. Rescheduling of a trial session requires at least 12 hours advance notification.

3. Tuition Fees & Billing Cycle

  • Tuition fees are payable in advance on a monthly, quarterly, or annual basis as specified in the student invoice.
  • Fee payments must be remitted by or before the 5th day of the billing cycle.
  • Tuition covers scheduled 1-on-1 instruction, use of campus facilities during allocated lesson hours, and institute study handouts.
  • External examination fees (such as Trinity College London, ABRSM, Rockschool, or Gandharva Mahavidyalaya exam entry fees) are set by the respective boards and are billed separately.

4. Attendance & Rescheduling Policy

To maintain pedagogical consistency and respect faculty schedules, the following rescheduling rules strictly apply:

  • 24-Hour Notice Required: If a student is unable to attend a scheduled private lesson, notification must be submitted to the Admissions Desk at least 24 hours in advance. A rescheduled makeup slot will be provided within 30 days.
  • Same-Day Cancellations & No-Shows: Lessons cancelled with less than 24 hours notice or missed without prior communication are forfeited and cannot be credited or refunded.
  • Faculty Unavailability: In the rare event an instructor is indisposed due to illness or performance obligations, a qualified substitute maestro or a makeup lesson will be arranged at the student's convenience.

5. Studio Conduct & Campus Etiquette

All students, parents, and visitors at our studio located at SCO 64-65, 2nd Floor, Sector 34-A, Chandigarh are expected to adhere to a standard of mutual respect, punctuality, and artistic care:

  • Instruments and equipment must be handled with utmost care. Intentional damage to pianos, amplifiers, audio consoles, or studio acoustics will incur repair costs.
  • Food and sugary beverages are strictly prohibited inside instrument practice suites and the recording booth. Bottled water is permitted.
  • Mobile phones must be placed on silent mode during all private lessons and masterclasses.

6. Intellectual Property

All proprietary teaching methodologies, customized exercises, sheet music transcriptions, audio backing tracks, and curriculum materials developed by Classic Music Institute are the intellectual property of Classic Music Institute. Reproduction, recording, or distribution for commercial purposes is strictly prohibited without prior written authorization.

7. Withdrawal & Refund Policy

Written notice of withdrawal must be submitted 15 calendar days before the start of a new billing month. Tuition fees for classes already delivered or reserved are non-refundable. Advance payments for future months may be credited toward future semesters or transferred to an immediate family member upon administrative approval.

8. Governing Law & Legal Jurisdiction

These Terms and Conditions are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising in connection with these terms or the services provided by Classic Music Institute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Chandigarh, India.
