Music Theory & Audio Production

Home / Services / Music Theory & Audio
Composition, Harmony & Modern Sound Engineering

From Musical Concept to Mastered Record

Great music is born at the intersection of harmonic theory and sonic engineering. Learning music theory unlocks the "why" behind the music you love, enabling you to compose original chord sequences, arrange multi-instrument arrangements, and pass Trinity / ABRSM Grade 5 Theory examinations (required for Grade 6+ practical exams).

At Classic Music Institute, our audio production laboratory at SCO 64-65, Sector 34-A Chandigarh features industry-standard Digital Audio Workstations (Logic Pro X, Ableton Live, FL Studio), Universal Audio interfaces, monitor speakers, and professional condenser microphones.

Core Subject Modules

Module 1: Graded Music Theory (Grades 1â€“8)

Time signatures, key signatures, Circle of Fifths, intervals, transposing, four-part vocal harmony, cadences, and full preparation for Trinity and ABRSM written examinations.

Module 2: DAW Architecture & MIDI

Navigating Logic Pro, Ableton Live, or FL Studio. MIDI programming, virtual software instruments, velocity tailoring, sample editing, and automation curves.

Module 3: Studio Recording & Microphones

Microphone polar patterns (cardioid, figure-8, omni), gain staging, pop filters, room acoustics, tracking live acoustic instruments, and tracking pristine vocals in our vocal booth.

Module 4: Mixing & Mastering Essentials

Parametric EQ carve-outs, dynamic range compression, sidechaining, spatial reverb & delay, stereo imaging, reference tracks, and LUFS loudness mastering for Spotify/Apple Music.
