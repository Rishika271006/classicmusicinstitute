Learning Pathways

Structured Four-Tier Progression

Our syllabus guides students progressively from foundational curiosity to professional conservatory virtuosity.

01. Foundation Tier (Months 1–6)

For pure beginners of all ages. Focuses on instrument anatomy, ergonomic hand/body posture, ear sensitivity, basic notation reading, and simple melodies.

02. Intermediate Tier (Months 6–18)

Expands technical repertoire, arpeggios, two-hand sync, dynamic articulation, and introduces Trinity / ABRSM Grade 1 to 3 examination pieces.

03. Advanced Tier (Months 18–36)

Covers complex rhythmic polyphony, advanced harmonic theory, expressive interpretation, and Trinity / ABRSM Grade 4 to 6 examination tracks.

04. Conservatory & Grade 8

Prepares prospective professionals, concert recitalists, and university applicants for Grade 7, Grade 8, and Associate Diplomas (ATCL/DipABRSM).
