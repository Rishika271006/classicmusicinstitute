About Classic Music Institute

Home / About Us
Our Heritage & Legacy

Cultivating Musical Virtuosity Since 2012

Founded with the belief that true music education transcends casual song reproduction, Classic Music Institute was established in the cultural heart of Chandigarh (SCO 64-65, Sector 34-A) to provide structured, conservatory-grade artistic education.

Whether a child stepping onto the piano bench for the first time or an accomplished vocalist preparing for Trinity College London Grade 8 exams, our academy provides an uncompromising standard of ear training, sight-reading discipline, ergonomic technique, and musical expression.

The Four Pillars

Our Pedagogical Foundation

📖

Rigorous Graded Curriculum

Aligned with the syllabus of Trinity College London, ABRSM, Rockschool UK, and Gandharva Mahavidyalaya for recognized milestones.

🧑‍🏫

1-on-1 Maestro Mentoring

Every lesson is private, allowing faculty to diagnose subtle hand tensions, vocal strain, and timing discrepancies immediately.

🎧

Ear & Theory Integration

We don’t just teach students what notes to play; we teach why chords resolve, how harmonic tension works, and how to transcribe music by ear.

🎭

Stage & Performance Readiness

Regular live open-mics, conservatory recitals, and studio tracking sessions train students to master nerves and perform with poise.

Sector 34-A Chandigarh Campus Facilities

Our 2nd Floor studio at SCO 64-65 provides an inspiring physical space designed by acoustic engineers for pristine sonic clarity.

🎹

Acoustic Pianos

Yamaha acoustic uprights & weighted 88-key hammer action keyboards.

🎙️

Vocal Isolation Booth

Deadened acoustics, Rode & Shure microphones for pristine vocal tracking.

🎻

Strings & Bowing Lab

Mirrored posture stations for ergonomic violin and guitar technique.

🥁

Percussion Chamber

Tuned Sheesham Tablas, Roland electronic drum kits and cajons.
